## 1. WHAT IT IS

**One node, one class, category `Fizgig`, MIT (real file, Peter Neill 2026), zero dependencies** — built on the NEW `comfy_api.latest` node API (`io.ComfyNode`/`io.Schema`/`comfy_entrypoint`; present at v0.37.4, `nodes.py:2306`). It is a **model patch** (after LoRAs, before the sampler) — not graph wiring, not conditioning, not sampler/sigma manipulation. `add_tweaks` clones the ModelPatcher, appends a shared tweak list into `transformer_options["fizgig_h3_tweaks"]`, and installs ONE dispatcher as `patches_replace("dit", "double_block", i)` on the union of target blocks (20–49). Per block, per step, the dispatcher either re-weights the **video-token slice of the block's residual update** (`d = out − in`, decomposed into spatial-frequency bands on the token grid) or swaps the block's attention module for a re-implementation with **text-row V scaling**. Two nodes chain additively; a foreign replace-patch on shared blocks is honestly noted then replaced (ComfyUI allows one per block).

| Control (node `FizgigH3Tweaks`) | Mechanism | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Detail & Contrast** (default +0.15, ±1.0) | `d_high = d − blur(d, 1 token)` — a 1-token-radius high-pass on the block's update to the video rows; `new = d + s·band` | blocks 40–49, last half of steps |
| **↳ mode** (default "stable across frames") | the high band projected onto its temporal mean — boosts only frame-stable detail (their measure: +7% shimmer @0.3 vs +20% per-frame; script unpublished). T=1 renders skip the projection (single frame) | — |
| **Scene Variation** (0, ±0.5) | `d_low = blur(d, 3 tokens)` — the LOW band, early steps: graded layout re-roll at fixed seed ("sub-seed"; same settings → same result) | blocks 20–49, first third of steps |
| **Prompt Strength** (0, −0.5..+3.0) | in attention: `v[:text_len] *= (1+s)` — text rows' VALUES scaled, K untouched (attention pattern unchanged; every video AND audio token takes proportionally more from the prompt). The block's own attention is swapped via the existing `attention=` override hook (`DiTBlock.forward`, v0.37.4 in-source) for `_PromptWeightedAttention`, a line-faithful re-implementation of `comfy.ldm.minimax.model.Attention.forward` | all blocks, all steps |
| **report** | console line per step/block (which tweaks fired where) — debug observability | — |

**Dropped en route (14-commit same-day churn, dead code retained):** a five-chainable-nodes design collapsed to one node; a whole-update FreeU-style gain was tried and **dropped** ("grainy by 0.2, broken by 0.35" — a published negative result); Motion (`d − temporal mean`) and Local Contrast (`blur1 − blur3`) bands dropped from the UI but live in `band()`; a Scene-Variation seed sub-dial added then dropped; **noise-level-based windows tried and reverted the same 8 minutes later** — the final windows are step-fractions read from the sampler's own schedule (`_step_index` from `sigmas`/`sample_sigmas`), scaling to any step count. Stale residue: a duplicated contradictory `WHERE` comment, dead `_steps()`, and a latent `"N+"` block-range path that silently discards the marker instead of expanding (unreachable from the UI — `WHERE` is fixed).

**What H3 behaviors it "tweaks":** fine texture/local contrast (late steps, deep blocks), layout variation (early steps), and prompt adherence (attention-level, CFG-free — H3 Turbo runs guidance 1.0, where any CFG>1 double-applies guidance and degrades [DOC]). Text and audio rows are never modified by the update tweaks (only the target `video` segment span is touched, verified against `PackedLayout.segments` at v0.37.4); the token grid is the DiT's 2×2 patchification (`gh, gw = lh//2, lw//2`, one token = 32 px at 512).

## 3. QUALITY / EVIDENCE

## 4. FIT — verdicts and test design (all PROPOSED-PENDING-TEST; the epistemology applies)

- **Prompt Strength — ADOPT-candidate, the headline. E-TW1:** the matched-arm design with **no incumbent on the axis** (turbo lanes have no adherence dial at guidance 1.0). **The killer arm: the 768p adherence-collapse rescue** — d98kth3 1b's strongest finding is that adherence collapses above ~576p and more steps do NOT repair structure; test whether text-V scaling (+0.2/+0.5) rescues adherence exactly there (it is the cheapest candidate rescue on the board). Arms: A incumbent turbo render; B ±strength at 576p (sanity); C +strength at the 768p collapse domain; D negative −0.3 (loosening direction). Golden domains: the prompt-adherence goldens + blind pairs; cost rows: attention-reimplementation overhead (wall-clock, VRAM — the out-of-place rope + full-forward re-impl), zero expected on the update-band path. Composability: XOR-clean with E-FS1 (model patch vs latent/decode seams), runs on turbo and full CFG alike (on CFG>1 it stacks with guidance — measure separately). Fallback ladder: no measurable adherence movement at non-degrading strengths → CONFIRM the incumbent doctrine (adherence is resolution-bound, ledger the negative); movement only at degrading strengths → NONE (a distortion knob, not an adherence knob); clean rescue at 768p → ADJUST the low-res-then-reference workaround doctrine (a cheaper rung on that ladder).
- **Detail & Contrast — ADOPT-candidate, secondary, sequenced BEHIND E-FS1.** Both land on "skin detail" — running concurrently with the decode bake-off confounds both. **E-TW2:** the E-FS1-winning decode ± {0.15, 0.3} on the portrait/skin-detail golden domains, blind pairs, cost rows (per-block conv + clone overhead, 10 blocks × half the steps — cheap). Fallback ladder: matches decode-side sharpness gains → prefer whichever is cheaper/removable-er under the modularity contract (a 60-line port vs a decode path); decode wins outright → keep the dial as a per-take adjust only; shimmer on video goldens → "stable across frames" mode arm before dropping.
- **Scene Variation — ADJUST (the takes/variants primitive). E-TW3, cheap falsifier:** (a) the determinism claim (same settings → same result, 2×); (b) graded monotonicity (±0.1/0.2/0.3 vs a full seed re-roll); (c) look-preservation metrics vs seed-jump (the claim "overall look stays" is unmeasured anywhere). If it holds, the workbench takes surface gains a graded variation control — a UX-level ADJUST regardless of whether the pack itself is ever installed by us.
- **Motion and Local Contrast bands — NONE** (dead UI; E-MD1 owns motion; the contrast band's job is folded into the high-band naming).
- **Composability caveats for every arm:** (1) block-level XOR — any other `double_block` replace-patch (none in our registry today; if the VDN chain option or a future Block Skip-class pack lands, run tweaks arms with it OFF; the pack prints an honest note when it displaces one); (2) **step-fraction windows are scheduler-relative, not sigma-absolute** (their own noise-level attempt was same-day reverted) — deterministic under our pinned recipes, but tweak strengths are not normalized across schedulers; (3) cache-skip architectures (VDN LongCache class) would silently skip patched steps — untested, keep such arms cache-OFF; (4) the E-FS1 arms themselves are unaffected (different seam).
